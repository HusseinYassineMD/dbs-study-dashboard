About This Dashboard

This dashboard integrates operational data from DBS Tracker.xlsx, Monthly Meeting Updates.xlsx, and PT Visit Tracker (DHA_PE4).xlsx, linking every participant record, visit milestone, recruitment source, genotype result, lab request, and dropout event into a single research operations view.

Use the sidebar to explore study progress, participant-level details, clinical workflows, and the full raw data catalog.
