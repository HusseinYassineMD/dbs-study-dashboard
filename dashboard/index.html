About This Dashboard

This dashboard integrates operational data from DBS Tracker.xlsx and Monthly Meeting Updates.xlsx, linking every participant record, visit milestone, recruitment source, genotype result, lab request, and dropout event into a single research operations view.

Use the sidebar to explore study progress, participant-level details, clinical workflows, and the full raw data catalog.
